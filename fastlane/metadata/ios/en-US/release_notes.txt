What's new in this release:

- Search looks through notes too, and shows name matches first.
- After the app locks, unlocking takes you back to the screen you were on, with your search and your place in the list.
- The vault list remembers the sort order you picked.
- The sync log shows when each line happened and which device it's about. Settings > Sync has a Sync now button.
- The app lets you know when a newer version is out.
- Sync starts as soon as the first device you invite joins.
- Devices connect for sync more reliably.

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your devices.
