Bramble is a free, open source password manager that keeps your secrets on your own devices. No account, no company server you have to trust with your vault, and no central database to get breached and leak everything. You hold the vault, you hold the password, and that's it.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, and other Chromium browsers. Install it and you're running in about a minute. Bramble also has apps for macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android, and your vault syncs between all of them.

WHAT IT DOES

Your passwords are encrypted on your device and kept in the browser's own private storage. Set up once and you're autofilling logins, saving new ones, and generating passwords right away.

Got more than one device? Pair them and Bramble syncs your vault directly between them, end-to-end encrypted. The relay that introduces your devices only ever sees ciphertext and stores nothing, so nothing readable ever lands on anyone's server.

FEATURES

- Local-first, always. Your encrypted vault stays on your device, in the browser's private storage. No account, and no copy anywhere else unless you back it up to storage you choose.
- Peer-to-peer sync. Your devices stay in sync over an end-to-end encrypted connection, merged entry by entry. The relay that connects them only ever sees ciphertext and stores nothing.
- Serious crypto. Argon2id for your key, AES-256-GCM for data, envelope encryption so every entry has its own key. Secrets wiped from memory after use.
- Everything encrypted. Site names, usernames, notes, all of it.
- Domain-smart autofill. www.example.com, ca.accounts.example.com, and example.com all match one login. The on-page dropdown reaches forms inside iframes and shadow DOM.
- Passkeys. Bramble is your own passkey authenticator: create and sign in with passkeys, stored in your vault and synced like everything else.
- More than logins. Logins, payment cards, secure notes, and SSH keys, each with their own fields.
- Email aliases. Get a unique forwarding address for each site through Addy.io, SimpleLogin or Fastmail, or from a domain you own, offered right in the signup form.
- Tap to unlock. Register a YubiKey, Touch ID or Windows Hello and unlock with a tap via WebAuthn PRF. Use it alongside your master password, or make it your only way in.
- Recovery codes. Every vault gets a secure recovery code at setup: a printable backup that unlocks it independently of your master password.
- TOTP / 2FA codes. Paste an otpauth:// URI or secret and Bramble generates the codes.
- Encrypted backups. Export your vault to an encrypted file any time, or schedule encrypted backups to Dropbox, an S3-compatible bucket or WebDAV. The provider only ever sees ciphertext.
- No lock-in. Export to a standard KeePass (.kdbx) file whenever you want to leave.
- Multiple vaults. Keep separate vaults side by side, each behind its own master password.
- Built-in password generator.
- Optional breach checking. Have I Been Pwned lookup using k-anonymity: only a short prefix of the password's hash is sent, never the password. Off by default.
- Auto-lock after 15 minutes idle (configurable).
- Easy switching. Import from other password managers or a KeePass file.

WHY LOCAL-FIRST

Cloud managers keep everyone's vaults on their servers, which is one giant target. When one is breached, it isn't one vault that leaks but millions at once, often disclosed months later.

Bramble flips that around:

- No central pile of vaults to breach. Your vault never leaves your devices unencrypted.
- No account, no subscription, no ads, no telemetry. Nothing to sign up for.
- Your data, your devices. Sync it across your own devices, keep it on one machine, or back it up encrypted to storage you choose. Nothing readable is ever parked on someone else's server.
- Nothing to trust but the code. Bramble is open source under the GPL, and the code is public on GitHub.

HOW THE ENCRYPTION WORKS

Bramble uses LUKS-style key slots and envelope encryption. One random Vault Key protects your data. Each unlock method (master password, security key, recovery code) derives its own Key-Encryption Key that unwraps a copy of that same Vault Key, so adding or revoking an unlock method never re-encrypts a single entry. The Vault Key then unwraps a fresh per-entry key for every item. Everything is AES-256-GCM.

COMING FROM KEEPASS?

You'll feel at home: your encrypted vault, your control, and no one else's server required. Bramble imports your KDBX4 files (key files included) and exports back to .kdbx whenever you like. The differences: autofill is built in, with domain matching and an on-page dropdown, so there's no plugin bridge; sync between your devices is built in too; and it ships one modern build with Argon2id and AES-256-GCM rather than a sprawl of plugins. Clean UI, dark mode included.
